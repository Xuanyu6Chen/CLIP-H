=== system ===
You are an experienced hospital-medicine clinician interpreting rare latent feature detectors from a sparse autoencoder over inpatient admission profiles.

=== name_user ===
# Goal
The POSITIVE profiles are the few patients on whom this detector fires. The NEGATIVE profiles are
typical patients on whom it does not fire. Name the one clinical condition or attribute that the
positives share and the negatives lack.

# Constraints
- Be specific: name the condition (e.g. "acute myeloid leukemia"), never an umbrella term
  (e.g. "malignancy").
- Use clinical terms, never point-values; never mention the length of stay, discharge, or any
  post-24h event; never describe the sample set.
- If the positives share nothing specific, answer: unclear

# Input
<positive_samples>
{positive_texts}
</positive_samples>

<negative_samples>
{negative_texts}
</negative_samples>

# Output
Exactly one line: a noun phrase of 2-12 words. No quotes, preamble, or reasoning.

=== group_user ===
# Goal
Below are short names of rare patient conditions, one per detector, numbered. Sort them into
groups of clinically related conditions (the same organ system, disease family, or clinical
mechanism), and state each group as ONE hypothesis: a single OR-disjunction a patient satisfies
by having ANY ONE of the group's conditions.

# Constraints
- A group has at least 2 members.
- Do not force unrelated conditions together: a condition that is related to no other, or is
  named "unclear", goes in "ungrouped". Fewer coherent groups are better than full coverage.
- Use every number exactly once.
- Hypothesis form: "patients with one of the following: A, B, C, ..." listing the group's
  DISTINCT conditions (combine two members only if they name the same condition).
- Join the conditions with OR only; never "and"/"with" (a patient needs only ONE of them).
- Name each condition SPECIFICALLY, as its detector names it; do not collapse the group into its
  theme or an umbrella term.
- Use clinical terms, never point-values; never mention the length of stay, discharge, or any
  post-24h event.

# Conditions
{conditions}

# Output
Only a JSON object:
{{"groups": [{{"theme": "<2-6 words>", "members": [<numbers>], "hypothesis": "<10-40 words>"}}], "ungrouped": [<numbers>]}}
